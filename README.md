**What are adhkar?**
The word *dhikr* (ذكر, plural *adhkar*) means “remembrance” or “mention.” In Islamic practice it means consciously remembering Allah through specific words taught in the Qur’an and the Sunnah (traditions of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ). Adhkar are not formal prayers like the five daily *salah*; they are informal, flexible recitations that can be done at any time — while walking, driving, working, or resting.

**Morning and evening adhkar (أذكار الصباح والمساء)**
The most famous category is the morning and evening remembrances. Muslims recite them after Fajr and after Asr (or at sunrise and sunset). They include:
– *Ayat al-Kursi* (Qur’an 2:255)
– *Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, and An-Nas* (the three “protective” surahs), recited three times
– *”Asbahna wa asbahal-mulku lillah…”* — “We have entered the morning and the dominion belongs to Allah…”
– *”Allahumma bika asbahna, wa bika amsayna…”* — asking God to be the sustainer of one’s day and night
– *”Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi”* (100 times) — “Glory be to Allah and praise Him”
– *”La ilaha illa Allah wahdahu la sharika lah…”* (10 or 100 times)

**Adhkar after prayer (أذكار بعد الصلاة)**
After each obligatory prayer, Muslims recite *Astaghfirullah* (3x), then *”Allahumma antas-salam wa minkas-salam…”*, then *Subhanallah* (33x), *Alhamdulillah* (33x), and *Allahu Akbar* (33x), completing 100 with *”La ilaha illa Allah wahdahu la sharika lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-hamd…”*

**Adhkar for protection and daily life**
There are adhkar for waking up, sleeping, eating, leaving the house, entering the house, travelling, entering the mosque, and times of distress. Notable ones include *”Bismillahil-ladhi la yadurru ma’as-mihi shay’un…”* (3x morning and evening), and *”Hasbunallahu wa ni’mal-wakil.”*

**Spiritual purpose**
The purpose of adhkar is to keep the heart connected to God throughout the day, reduce anxiety, increase gratitude, seek protection from harm and evil, and earn reward. The Qur’an says: *”Verily, in the remembrance of Allah do hearts find rest”* (13:28).
